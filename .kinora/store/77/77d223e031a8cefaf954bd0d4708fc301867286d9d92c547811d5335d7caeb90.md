# Spec — on a narrow screen the console shows one pane at a time, drilled into

**Contract.** Below a width threshold (a phone held upright) the console
shows exactly **one** pane, chosen by the selection: nothing selected shows
the list; a selected context shows its head and trace (a tab between them);
a selected node shows its jobs; a selected job or taken-over pane shows its
output or screen. A back control (and the phone's own back gesture, see the
URL spec) steps out one level. Above the threshold the same panes sit side
by side as before. Which arrangement applies is part of the layout value
(kino://ed06836e3296e0ea5caad3e2ca7a972c911743ad1a4f87f149c069132dadac35), decided by a stylesheet media query — no component knows
the screen width.

**Chosen.** A full-screen drill: each pane gets the whole phone, the
approvals badge stays in the top bar on every level.

**Rejected.** The stacked column (long scrolls to reach the trace); bottom
tabs (five tabs do not fit, and the drill already names where you are).

**Escape hatch.** A pane that needs both list and detail on a phone shows
the detail as its own drill level.
