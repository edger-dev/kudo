# Decision — keep the dogfood inbox until triage is proven, and do not install global hooks

The personal inbox keeps policy `never` until at least one complete triage demonstrates that entries are assigned to tasks or explicitly marked won't. MaxAge may be reconsidered after that, not before.

Kudo does not install a global SessionStart hook to detect bare CLI sessions. Its hooks stay inside the worker launcher; for the dogfood week, bare-session friction is recorded deliberately rather than by modifying unrelated CLI sessions. A wrapper-only counter is also rejected because it misses ordinary CLI starts while appearing to measure them.

The existing decisions stand: worker `inbox` captures rough edges, and the dogfood week's record is its own context with measures in submissions. The console measures view remains planned, not implemented by this decision. No code or spec change is required now.
