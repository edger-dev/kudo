# Spec — a queue item may carry a due time, and is not ready before it

**Contract.** An item may be enqueued with a **due time** (epoch seconds, as
the item's metadata). Before it, the item is not ready: `pull` and the Stop
hook's handoff skip it, ready counts leave it out, and a waiting worker keeps
waiting. From its due time on it is ready, and goes before items with no due
time (it was scheduled), the oldest due first. The console shows a not-yet-due item with its time.

**Chosen.** Due-ness is a property of the item, decided by the same `ready`
rule every reader uses, so the worker, the console and the waker cannot
disagree about what is due. Items stay write-once: a time is fixed at enqueue.

**Rejected.** A separate schedule beside the queue (a second source of truth
for what runs next). Moving an item's due time in place (items are write-once;
a new time is a new item, as a check's snooze makes one).

**Escape hatch.** An item enqueued with no due time is ready at once, as
today.
