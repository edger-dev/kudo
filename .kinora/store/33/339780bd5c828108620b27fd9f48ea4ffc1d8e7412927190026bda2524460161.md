# Finding, 21 Oct 2026: two contexts with one title, born in one second, are one context

A context's identity is the hash of its card, and the card holds only the
title and the creation time to the second
(`# <title>\n\nkudo context, created <ts>\n`). Two `create_context` calls
with the same title in the same second write the same card: one identity,
and the second call's first head becomes a fork of the first's.

By hand this is unlikely; anything that creates contexts by script (the
console, a node, a test) will hit it. Fix: put something unique in the card,
as items already do with their marker line — a random nonce, or the
creator's lease or node plus a counter — and test that two same-title,
same-second creates give two ids.
