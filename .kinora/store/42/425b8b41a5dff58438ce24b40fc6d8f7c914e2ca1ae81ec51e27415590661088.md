# Finding — a pane whose job ended before its lease traced lists as "starting" forever

startup prompt never traced a lease-start, so it stays on its context's card
as a *starting* worker with a pane to take over, long after its job ended.

job lives. Fix: give the pane record its time and show it quiet past a
claim's life (as traced leases are), or have the console check the job's
status through the hub's job list when it reads the panes.
