# Spec — the pull loop writes its own trace

**Contract.** Every lease writes a **trace** into the context store as it
runs: one write-once kino per loop event, written by the hooks and tools
themselves, never by the model. The events are the loop's own — the lease
starting, an item handed over or pulled (its claim), a stop refused while a
claim is open, a claim released, a submit (its submission), and the lease
ending with its reason (queue empty, compaction, budget, takeover, stuck).
The trace of a lease reads in order; the trace of a context is its leases'
traces together.

From any head version the trace runs **forward** to what produced it: the
submission that wrote it, that submission's claim, the item it answered, and
the lease that held the claim — whose trace shows the rest of that session's
work. Transcript spans join this chain when transcripts reach the store
(phase 3).

**Chosen.** A mechanical floor under the authored submission, kept apart from
it: the trace says what happened, the submission says what it means. A
submission that claims more than its lease's trace supports is visible
without a judge. Two kinos rather than one, so the authored part can be
rewritten by a later observer without touching the record of events.

**Rejected.** Folding the events into the submission (the worker would author
its own audit trail). Inferring the events later from the transcript (the
loop knows them by construction; inference is the fallback the pull seam
removed). Keeping them only in the per-lease state file (local to one
machine, gone with it, and invisible to the console).

**Escape hatch.** Tool-call counts and the files and repos a lease touched
come from a PostToolUse hook as further trace events, behind the same shape,
when they are needed; until then the trace is the loop's events alone.
