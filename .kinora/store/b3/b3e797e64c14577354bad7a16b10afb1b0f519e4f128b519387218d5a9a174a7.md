Decided (author, 22 Oct): a waker service on one node; checks are one-shot
with snooze; the verdict is typed in the submission; a context declares its
wake node. Each step tests → implementation → fresh review → fixes.

## Steps

1. **The store.** Items take a due time (metadata), and `ready` skips the
   not-yet-due; check items carry what / pass / window; a submission on a
   check needs a verdict (pass / fail / snooze until) and a note, refused
   otherwise; a snooze enqueues the check again, due then, naming its
   origin; the wake-node declaration. CLI: `context enqueue --due`,
   `context check`, `context wake-node`.
2. **The worker.** The claim text shows a check's condition and window;
   `submit` takes the verdict; the hook's handoff and the wait respect due
   times.
3. **The waker.** `kudo-shim waker`: once a minute, contexts with a newly due
   item and no running worker get one on their wake node, through the typed
   start, the pane recorded; tested against a stand-in starter.
4. **The console.** Not-yet-due items show with their time; a check's verdicts
   read in the log.
5. **Live.** The waker as a user service beside the console (dotfiles); a
   check queued minutes ahead wakes a worker on yolo and closes with a
   verdict.
