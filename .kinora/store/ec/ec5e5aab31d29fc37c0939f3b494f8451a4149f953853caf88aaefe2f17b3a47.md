# Decision, 24 Oct 2026: the byte substrate — shape and home (kura design, 1 of 3)

Designs kino://4e307377e8cffb5a701e86f96340cdbf37db43d28755f6819194c4764779345d (the object-store backend in moco).

## Chosen

- **Home:** framework-free crates in moco, straight away (not trialled in
  kinora first). moco uses kinora only as a jig tool, so kinora depending on
  them makes no cargo cycle.
- **Shape:** one trait under ledger and media alike, every device working
  through a mandatory local place. Specs in moco's ledger:
  - `moco:a7089331ac0dff49deb5acc9afce92b2a9d8a9d5022f66af915f3a2332d6f447` — one streaming `Place` trait; caller hashes first
  - `moco:95cf2a2fda557b922e0e3ff0ddaf48c25d7e598de9358cb53985b8f1aa7b3aa0` — meeting point + hub store connector; removal never over the wire
  - `moco:7778729bc8018fe949b0225f90ad2832501a9e82b9d03c973d10bdae568f7c8b` — place-owned inventories (hash, size, last verified)
  - `moco:7d189e690068d0f7200fcdbb236afe6ab64b6ad6535c8a8a883c3f825b72c79a` — local working copy; done locally, safe by inventories
  - `moco:c08aa5551e04cf03a5f4df25c0f647b02dd78d7eacfb3872cb197affc6b52d2a` — mutable state as append-only streams
  - and in kinora's: `kinora:02be1c37832e1c710030e7bc5a89441b55bdbf5a05ebe2c501f824708b7f3372` — root versions as a stream; commit no longer main-only

## Consequences

- RFC-0005 amended: the ledger goes behind the trait too, through the working
  copy; its "ledger in central mode" question is answered by streams.
- When the work starts, re-version in kinora: the mirror spec (it rejected a
  trait) and the content-addressing extraction spec (its home is now moco).
  `kinora-remote` becomes moco's object-store place.
- The second kura question (where presence is tracked) is answered by the
  inventory spec.

## Open

- Chunking very large files (multi-GB video) behind the streaming trait.
- The search model at kura scale (kura design 3 of 3).
