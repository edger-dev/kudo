# Spec — a check closes with pass, fail or snooze, typed in the submission

**Contract.** A **check** is a queue item that says what to look at, a
**measurable pass condition** and a fixed **evidence window**, usually with a
due time (kino://716432a845eb22babf957938aaa9a9c0f3cd3bea3ebee24c89a5147a795e7e2b). Submitting a check's claim must carry a
**verdict** — `pass`, `fail`, or `snooze` with the time to look again — and a
note; a check submitted without one is refused, and a verdict on an ordinary
item is refused. `pass` and `fail` close the check. `snooze` closes this item
and enqueues the same check again, due at the time given, naming the item it
came from. The verdict is written into the submission (its body and its
metadata), so the context's log is the append-only check log.

**Chosen.** Modelled on the notes vault's deferred checks (an append-only log,
a measurable pass condition, a fixed evidence window, closed with pass / fail
/ snooze and a note). One write path: a check is worked like any item and
closes through the same submission, with the head updated as usual.
Recurrence is a snooze every time — nothing repeats on its own.

**Rejected.** A separate check-log kino beside the submission (a second write
path). A repeat interval on the check (more to build, and something to stop).

**Escape hatch.** A check whose condition needs a human is answered by the
worker as `snooze` with a note, or failed with the reason.
