## Phase 2 — local receiver · done

Contract: `intake-local-receiver` (id
9039f4f2be3b4e526c5986cb678438facf2b33a3bc5c7136b363dbc6204dadb4).
The receiver resolves aliases to clean dedicated `main` checkouts, validates
RFC 3339 times, stores provenance and unreviewed metadata, compacts Kinora,
makes local Git commits, and lists arrivals by receipt time. Stable note ids
and content fingerprints deduplicate retries across every root, including after
triage, and conflicting reuse is refused. The Kinora pin moved to `9d6e5c5` for
root streams. Real temporary Git/Kinora tests cover delivery, identical content
under distinct ids, retries, conflicts, dirty and detached checkouts, triage,
and time filtering. Implementation: `a192217`.
