# Spec — a worker's shell calls and jobs are recorded, and may be cited

**Contract.** A worker's PostToolUse hook records every **shell call** it
makes — the command, its exit, and the first and last 2 KB of its output —
as a write-once kino in its lease's trace (`tool-call`). A governed job's
start and end, already traced (`job-start`, `job`), are recorded the same
way. A submission may cite either as evidence for an established line
(kino://4e67251bc978f34e7d6ce4278eb1503f9e803e51f0057d4d5954c701830f22b9): the record is the loop's own, written by the hook as
the call returned, not the worker's later account of it. Reads, edits and
other tools are not recorded. A failed shell call is recorded too, with its
error (PostToolUseFailure). The record is written to the local store only —
a shell call never waits on the network — and the next sync carries it to the
mirror.

The record is **tamper-evident, not tamper-proof**: the worker runs as the
store's own user, so it could write into the store by other means. A call
that runs the hook itself is not recorded (the call that ran it is, and shows
it); a stronger binding (the call's id checked against the session
transcript) waits for transcripts in the store.

**Chosen.** A check's result is nearly always "I ran X and saw Y"; recording
the call where it happens turns that into evidence without trusting the
worker's word, and the hook trace is already the loop's record. Shell and
jobs only keeps the store's growth to what checks cite.

**Rejected.** An `observe` tool the worker calls with what it ran and saw (its
own report, not a record). Accepting every check as claimed (the normal case
would read as the exception). Recording every tool call (the store grows
fastest for evidence nobody cites).

**Escape hatch.** Transcript spans, when transcripts reach the store, cite
anything else a session saw.
