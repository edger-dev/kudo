## Done-when

- `context check` enqueues a check (what, pass condition, evidence window,
  due); `context enqueue --due` a due item; neither is pulled before its time.
- A worker's `submit` on a check carries the verdict and note; a snooze
  re-queues it due later; the log reads as the check log.
- `kudo-shim waker` wakes a worker on the context's wake node when a check
  comes due and no worker runs; it never wakes for ordinary items.
- Live on the home lab: a check queued for a few minutes ahead wakes a worker
  on yolo at its time and closes with a verdict, seen from the phone.
