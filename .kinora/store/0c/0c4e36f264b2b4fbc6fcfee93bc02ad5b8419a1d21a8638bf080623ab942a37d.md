# A pane that never traced goes quiet, then leaves the console

**Chosen.** A worker pane the console recorded but whose lease never traced
anything shows as *starting* for its first 10 minutes, as *starting, quiet*
after that (still offered for take over — a CLI may sit at a prompt nobody
answered), and is no longer listed once it is 24 hours old.

**Why.** A worker's SessionStart traces within seconds of its CLI starting;
one silent for minutes is stuck at a prompt or dead (its job killed, its node
restarted). Listed forever as *starting*, dead panes buried the live ones.

**Rejected.** Asking every node whether each job still runs on every render
(a hub round trip per pane, and a node that is down would hide its panes).
Dropping at 10 minutes (a pane waiting on a human would vanish).

**Escape hatch.** The pane records stay in the store; only the listing ages
them out.
