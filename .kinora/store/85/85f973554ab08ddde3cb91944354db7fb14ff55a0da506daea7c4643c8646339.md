# Spec — an approvals badge tells a trustworthy zero from unknown, and opens a tray

**Contract.** A badge is always visible in the top bar with three states: a
count of jobs waiting for the author's verdict (amber) when any are queued;
**unknown** (red, naming how many nodes) when nothing is queued but a node's
queue could not be read; **none** (muted) only when every node answered and
nothing waits. A real count wins over trouble. Tapping it opens a tray over
the page with every waiting job, oldest first and in a stable order (rows
never move under a finger), the nodes that could not be read named in red,
and the outcome of the last verdict. The tray stays open after a verdict,
even when the queue empties, so a failed verdict never looks like success;
Esc, the close control or back closes it.

**Chosen.** The earlier console's badge and tray, kept exactly, replacing the
"waiting for you" block at the top of the *where* pane.

**Rejected.** The queue inline in the list (on a phone it pushes the list off
screen, and is gone once you drill in); a count that reads zero when a node
is unreachable.

**Escape hatch.** A notification channel (push to the phone) can later
point at the same tray.
