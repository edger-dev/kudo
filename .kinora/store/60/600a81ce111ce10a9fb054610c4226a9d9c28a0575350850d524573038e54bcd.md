## Phase 1 — the shim without a hub

A worker session needs the context store, not the hub. The shim starts
without `KUDO_HUB_ADDR` (or with a hub that does not answer): the worker
tools and hooks work, each job tool answers that no hub is configured or
reachable. A worker session with a hub is unchanged.
